A single one-line, 1-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner — under 280 characters — for the [make.fun bounty "One Star, Written in Sap"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4).

## Entry

> One star. He waters me with cold coffee, hides me behind the TV for 'ambience,' then calls me his thriving jungle. I've seen sunlight twice and I'm now interviewing new owners.

176 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only, Python 3.13, deterministic, no network) writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and validates it: 1–279 characters, single line, not whitespace-only.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
